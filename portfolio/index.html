About me

hi, I’m Palak

just another science kid who graduated in BCA - which, in a lot of rooms, isn’t exactly the “expected” background - but didn’t stop me from showing up anyway.

first real job was at KPMG, sitting next to B.Com grads and figuring out private equity, Excel, and a bunch of things nobody tells you until you mess them up once. Learned a lot the hard way, and honestly, that’s usually how it sticks.

then stumbled into product management and just... got hooked. It’s this perfect overlap of tech, creativity, problem-solving, and people - exactly where i shine and everything I’m into, in one place. i have got some teardowns and case studies in the works.

also giving cybersecurity another go - certifications, labs, actually understanding the field instead of just skimming it.

and somewhere in the middle of all this, i ended up doing a little bit of everything for a candle brand - digital presence, onboarding clients like JW Marriott and SMOOR, manufacturing, photography, videography, the works. currently obsessing over a gtm strategy for them. this is honestly where I understood what being a “generalist” even means.

my path’s been pretty non-linear, and it was never supposed to be linear. i’m a multitasker, a quick learner, and once I start something, I don’t quit on it.

still figuring a lot of this out as I go - but i’m curious enough to keep exploring, and stubborn enough to keep trying

Projects

Fake News Detection

Machine Learning Model

Reviewed research papers and articles, used an entertainment-based news dataset called GossipCop, and evaluated several ML algorithms to find the best-performing model — Logistic Regression, at ~80% accuracy.

AI Gut Health Companion — Multi Signal Wellness Tracker

Personalised Morning Ritual Feature

Created a personalised morning ritual feature for an Indian gut health brand by documenting the user problem and data signals to track, including sleep quality, stress levels, bowel movement logs, and meal logs. Developed a low-fidelity prototype using Figma, planned A/B testing, and documented key metrics such as time spent in morning rituals, return rate, reduction in GI issues, and the correlation between consistent rituals and improved bowel movement logs.

Recruitment Strategy: Sourcing, Funnel Analysis & Referrals 2.0

Talent Acquisition & Hiring Process Optimisation

Evaluated candidates for Software Development Engineer (SDE) and Technical Program Manager (TPM) roles, focusing on AI fluency, domain expertise, and growth potential beyond keyword matching. Analysed gaps in the hiring funnel and proposed a simple Yes/Maybe/No candidate-labeling system to reduce unnecessary interviews and improve recruiter–hiring manager alignment. Redesigned the employee referral process by proposing AI-powered referral suggestions, simplified one-click forms, and expanded eligibility to interns, freelancers, and alumni.
